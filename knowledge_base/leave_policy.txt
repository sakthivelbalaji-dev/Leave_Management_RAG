LEAVE BALANCE

Leave Management System maintains employee leave balances.

Formula: Available Days = Allocated Days - Used Days

An employee cannot normally submit a leave request when the available
balance is insufficient for the requested duration.

Example: Allocated Days = 12 Used Days = 4 Available Days = 12 - 4 = 8
days

Explanation: Personal leave balances must come from the authenticated

A submitted request has status Pending until reviewed.

================================================================
A. CASUAL LEAVE
================================================================

Purpose:
Casual Leave is intended for short-term personal needs, planned personal
work, family functions, appointments, urgent personal matters, or other
ordinary situations where the employee needs a short absence from work.

Typical situations where Casual Leave may be requested:
- Attending a family function.
- Attending a wedding.
- Handling personal work.
- Attending a personal appointment.
- Taking care of an unexpected personal matter.
- Taking a short break for a personal commitment.
- Attending a school or family-related event.
- Handling a non-medical household or personal requirement.

Example:
"I need casual leave on 19-09-2026 because I have a family function."

The AI should extract:
Leave Type = Casual Leave
Start Date = 2026-09-19
End Date = 2026-09-19
Reason = family function

Demonstration entitlement:
Up to 12 days per calendar year.

Demonstration usage rule:
Casual Leave may normally be requested for one or more consecutive days,
subject to available balance and manager approval.

Demonstration restriction:
Casual Leave should not be used as a substitute for Sick Leave when the
employee is genuinely unable to work because of illness, where the
appropriate sick-leave category is available.

Documentation:
Normally no medical document is required for ordinary personal reasons,
but the manager or HR may request clarification when appropriate.

Approval:
Casual Leave is Pending after submission and remains unapproved until the
assigned manager or authorized reviewer approves it.

================================================================
B. SICK LEAVE
================================================================

Purpose:
Sick Leave is intended for situations where an employee is unable to work
because of illness, injury, medical treatment, medical appointments, or
recovery from a health-related condition.

Typical situations:
- Fever or flu.
- Cold or infection.
- Stomach illness.
- Injury that prevents working.
- Doctor or hospital appointment.
- Medical examination.
- Recovery after a medical procedure.
- Other genuine health-related situations.

Examples:
"I need sick leave tomorrow because I have a doctor's appointment."
"I am unwell and need sick leave today."
"I need two days of sick leave because I have a fever."

Demonstration entitlement:
Up to 12 days per calendar year.

Demonstration usage rule:
Sick Leave may be requested for one or more days when the employee is
unable to work due to a health-related reason.

Documentation:
For extended absence, HR or the manager may request supporting medical
documentation. This demonstration policy does not define a fixed number
of days after which documentation becomes mandatory.

Important:
The AI must not request sensitive medical details unnecessarily.
The employee only needs to provide an appropriate leave reason for the
application.

- Planned rest period.
- Personal commitments requiring several days.

I. COMPENSATORY LEAVE
================================================================

Purpose:
Compensatory Leave is intended as time off provided where an employee has
worked additional hours or on an approved non-standard working day and
the applicable company process allows compensatory time.

Typical situations:
- Approved work on a scheduled weekly off.
- Approved work on a company holiday.
- Approved additional work that qualifies under company rules.

Examples:
"I worked on Saturday with manager approval and want compensatory leave."
"I worked on the approved holiday and want to use my compensatory day."

Demonstration entitlement:
Determined according to approved additional work and company records.

Important:
Compensatory Leave must not be automatically granted merely because an
employee worked extra hours. The additional work must qualify under the
applicable company process.

Database requirement:
The system should verify the employee's actual compensatory balance or
eligibility where such data is stored.

LEAVE TYPE DECISION GUIDE
================================================================

If the employee needs leave because of ordinary personal work:
Consider Casual Leave.

If the employee is sick, injured, receiving medical treatment, or has a
medical appointment:
Consider Sick Leave.

If the employee wants a planned vacation or longer personal break:
Consider Earned Leave or Annual Leave according to the employee's actual
configured leave types and balance.

If the employee has an unexpected urgent personal/family situation:
Consider Emergency Leave.

If the employee needs leave related to pregnancy, childbirth, or recovery:
Consider Maternity Leave.

If the employee needs leave following the birth or qualifying arrival of
a child as an eligible parent:
Consider Paternity Leave.

If the employee has experienced a qualifying death or serious family
circumstance:
Consider Bereavement / Compassionate Leave.

If the employee performed qualifying approved additional work:
Consider Compensatory Leave.

IMPORTANT:
This guide helps the AI understand the intended use of categories.
The final available leave type must be validated against the actual
database configuration.

AI RULE FOR AMBIGUOUS LEAVE TYPES
================================================================

If the user provides enough information to identify the leave type, do not
ask the leave type again.

Example:
"I want sick leave tomorrow because I have a fever."

The AI already knows:
Leave Type = Sick Leave
Date = tomorrow
Reason = fever

It should proceed to confirmation after validation.

If the user says:
"I need leave tomorrow because I have some personal work."

The likely category is Casual Leave, but the AI should validate against
the configured leave types and policy.

If more than one category could reasonably apply, the AI may ask:
"Would you like to use Casual Leave or another available leave category?"

The AI must not silently choose a category when the distinction materially
affects the employee's balance or eligibility.
