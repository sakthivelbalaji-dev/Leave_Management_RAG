============================================================
1. PURPOSE
============================================================

This policy defines the rules and procedures for employee working hours,
attendance, leave entitlement, leave application, leave approval,
attendance correction, work from home, employee complaints, grievances,
escalation and workplace responsibilities.

============================================================
2. SCOPE
============================================================

This policy applies to employees working at KnackForge, including:

- Full-time employees
- Part-time employees
- Probationary employees
- Contract employees
- Interns
- Other employees where applicable

Specific benefits or leave provisions may differ according to employment
category, employment agreement or applicable law.

============================================================
3. STANDARD WORKING HOURS
============================================================

Standard Working Days:

Monday to Friday

Standard Office Start Time:

9:00 AM

Standard Office End Time:

6:00 PM

Standard Lunch Break:

1:00 PM to 2:00 PM

Expected Working Hours:

8 working hours per standard working day.

Employees are expected to be available and actively working during their
scheduled working hours.

Employees assigned to a different shift must follow the working hours
approved for their department, project or employment agreement.

============================================================
4. OFFICE ATTENDANCE
============================================================

Employees are expected to maintain regular and punctual attendance.

Employees must:

- Report to work according to their assigned schedule.
- Complete the required working hours.
- Follow the company's attendance recording procedure.
- Inform their manager if they cannot attend work.
- Apply for appropriate leave when they are absent.
- Inform their manager about unexpected delays or absence.
- Maintain accurate attendance records.

Employees must not intentionally record attendance for another employee.

Attendance records must represent the employee's actual attendance.

============================================================
5. ATTENDANCE RECORDING
============================================================

Employees are responsible for recording their attendance using the
attendance system provided by the company.

Employees should verify that their attendance has been recorded correctly.

If attendance is missing or incorrect, the employee should contact their
manager or Human Resources as soon as possible.

============================================================
6. LATE ARRIVAL
============================================================

Employees are expected to arrive and begin work at their scheduled start
time.

Standard office start time is 9:00 AM.

If an employee expects to arrive late, the employee should inform their
manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Repeated late arrival may require discussion with the employee's manager
and Human Resources.

Late arrival should not be treated as approved leave unless the appropriate
leave or attendance adjustment has been approved.

============================================================
7. EARLY DEPARTURE
============================================================

Employees should not leave work before completing their scheduled working
hours without approval or authorization.

If an employee needs to leave early, they should:

1. Inform the manager.
2. Obtain approval where required.
3. Apply for the appropriate leave or attendance adjustment.

============================================================
8. BREAK POLICY
============================================================

Employees are entitled to the standard lunch break from:

1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.

Employees may take reasonable short breaks during the working day
according to team and workplace requirements.

Breaks must not interfere with:

- Customer commitments
- Meetings
- Project deadlines
- Team responsibilities
- Required working hours

============================================================
9. WORK FROM HOME
============================================================

Work from home is permitted only when approved by the appropriate manager
or when specifically allowed under company policy.

Employees working remotely must:

- Be available during scheduled working hours.
- Attend required meetings.
- Complete assigned work.
- Maintain communication with their team.
- Follow attendance requirements.
- Protect company information and equipment.

Working from home without required approval does not automatically
constitute approved leave or authorized remote work.

============================================================
10. GENERAL LEAVE POLICY
============================================================

Employees may request leave when they need time away from work.

Leave must normally be requested through the company's Leave Management
System.

============================================================
11. LEAVE TYPES
============================================================

The Leave Management System may contain the following leave categories:

- Casual Leave
- Sick Leave
- Earned Leave
- Annual Leave
- Emergency Leave
- Maternity Leave
- Paternity Leave
- Bereavement Leave
- Compensatory Leave
- Other approved leave categories

The exact leave types available to an employee are determined by the
active leave types configured in the Leave Management System and applicable
company rules.

============================================================
12. LEAVE ELIGIBILITY
============================================================

Leave eligibility may depend on:

- Employee status
- Employment category
- Applicable leave type
- Company policy
- Employment agreement
- Available leave balance

Employees should check their current leave balance before applying.

If the required leave type is unavailable or the balance is insufficient,
the employee should contact Human Resources.

============================================================
13. LEAVE BALANCE
============================================================

The Leave Management System maintains employee leave balances.

Available Leave is calculated as:

Available Days = Allocated Days - Used Days

An employee cannot normally submit a leave request when the available
balance is insufficient for the requested duration.

============================================================
14. HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
============================================================

Employees must use the Leave Management System to apply for leave.

General procedure:

1. Log in to the Leave Management System.
2. Open the Leave Request section.
3. Select the required leave type.
4. Select the start date.
5. Select the end date.
6. Enter the reason for leave.
7. Review the requested dates.
8. Verify that sufficient leave balance is available.
9. Submit the leave request.
10. Wait for the manager or authorized reviewer to approve or reject
    the request.

A submitted request has a status of:

Pending

until it is reviewed.

============================================================
15. LEAVE REQUEST STATUS
============================================================

A leave request may have one of the following statuses:

Pending:
The request has been submitted and is waiting for review.

Approved:
The authorized manager or reviewer has approved the request.

Rejected:
The authorized manager or reviewer has rejected the request.

Employees should check the Leave Management System for the current status
of their requests.

============================================================
16. LEAVE APPROVAL PROCESS
============================================================

Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's assigned manager or another
authorized reviewer.

Normal process:

Employee
    |
    v
Submit Leave Request
    |
    v
Pending
    |
    v
Manager Review
    |
    +-------------------+
    |                   |
    v                   v
Approved            Rejected

The manager may provide a comment when approving or rejecting the request.

============================================================
17. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing employee leave requests.
- Checking team availability.
- Considering project requirements.
- Approving valid leave requests.
- Rejecting requests when appropriate.
- Providing comments when necessary.
- Ensuring leave decisions are handled consistently.
- Monitoring employee attendance and leave patterns.

============================================================
18. MATERNITY LEAVE
============================================================

Maternity leave may be available according to company policy and applicable
employment rules.

Employees requiring maternity leave should contact Human Resources for
eligibility, documentation and duration requirements.

Where configured, the employee should submit the leave request through the
Leave Management System.

============================================================
19. PATERNITY LEAVE
============================================================

Paternity leave may be available according to company policy and applicable
employment rules.

Employees requiring paternity leave should contact Human Resources for
eligibility, documentation and duration requirements.

Where configured, the employee should submit the leave request through the
Leave Management System.

============================================================
20. BEREAVEMENT OR COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
============================================================

Employees may request bereavement or compassionate leave in the event of a
qualifying family or personal circumstance.

Eligibility and duration are determined according to applicable company
policy.

Employees should contact Human Resources when such leave is required.

============================================================
21. LEAVE CANCELLATION
============================================================

Employees who no longer require approved leave should inform their manager
as soon as possible.

If the Leave Management System provides a cancellation option, the employee
should use that option.

If cancellation is not available through the system, the employee should
contact the manager or Human Resources.

Cancellation of approved leave may require manager approval.

============================================================
22. LEAVE MODIFICATION
============================================================

If an employee needs to change the dates of an existing leave request, the
employee should contact the manager or follow the available modification
process in the Leave Management System.

A modified leave request may require a new approval.

============================================================
23. RETURN FROM LEAVE
============================================================

Employees are expected to return to work on the approved return date.

If an employee needs to extend the leave period, the employee should
contact the manager as soon as possible.

Additional leave is subject to availability, eligibility and approval.

============================================================
24. UNAPPROVED ABSENCE
============================================================

An absence without required notification or approval may be considered an
unapproved absence.

Employees should inform their manager when they cannot attend work.

Repeated unapproved absence may result in review by management and Human
Resources.

============================================================
25. WORKPLACE RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

Employees are expected to:

- Follow approved working hours.
- Maintain accurate attendance.
- Apply for leave through the Leave Management System.
- Provide truthful information in leave requests.
- Inform managers about unexpected absence.
- Follow approval requirements.

============================================================
26. HR SUPPORT
============================================================

Employees should contact the Human Resources Department when they need
clarification about eligibility, documentation, employment-specific rules,
or a policy provision that is not clearly covered in this document.

28. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

Q: What are the company working hours?

A: Standard working days are Monday to Friday. Standard office hours are
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a standard lunch break from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.
Expected working time is 8 hours per standard working day.

Q: How do I apply for leave?

A: Use the Leave Management System, select the leave type, select the start
and end dates, enter the reason, review the request, verify sufficient
balance, and submit it for manager or authorized-reviewer approval.

Q: What happens after I submit leave?

A: The request is Pending until it is reviewed.

Q: Can I work from home without approval?

A: No. Work from home is permitted only when approved by the appropriate
manager or when specifically allowed by company policy.

Q: What should I do if I will be late?

A: Inform your manager as soon as reasonably possible.
